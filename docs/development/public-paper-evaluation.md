# Real public-paper import and grounded-question evaluation

2026-11-04. Local Pivot increment; no public/VPS deployment or real mail. [Corpus, licences and exact reproduction](../../evals/public_documents/README.md), [spec016](../../specs/016-public-paper-evaluation.md).

## Scope and source truth

Three real papers, six files: one native eight-author Word manuscript plus its explicitly labelled PDF export; two publisher PDFs plus explicitly labelled Word text derivatives of their publisher JATS. The native Word is a public unpublished manuscript, the two PLOS pieces are a published editorial and essay. We do not claim six author originals or three peer-reviewed experimental studies. Source links, article-specific licence statements, exact SHA256 and byline gold values are in the corpus manifest. The small redistributed derivatives retain attribution and their source licences; originals and all evaluation/runtime records remain in ignored artifacts.

Gold data comes from the actual byline, numbered institutions and author contact blocks/publisher JATS. Registry contributor lists, editor Philip E. Bourne, reference authors and registry ORCIDs were not silently inserted into paper authors. Paper-year affiliations/contact addresses are not present-day verification. The editorial has no labelled abstract; its required review-form summary is explicitly editor-added. Ioannidis' abstract is taken from the publisher's Summary, not invented by the model.

## Findings and fixes

1. Every initial real Word file was rejected by blanket rules: legal internal `../customXml` part references and saved EndNote fields were treated as unsafe. OPC targets now resolve strictly within the archive namespace; absolute ZIP entries, escaping paths, external templates/images, macros and embeddings remain rejected. Only cached PAGE/NUMPAGES/EndNote EN.CITE/EN.CITE.DATA/EN.REFLIST results are read; no fields or plugins run/update. Missing citation results or unknown executable fields fail. Inert local PDF initial-page arrays are ignored; action dictionaries/scripts remain blocked. Parser contract is v2; old immutable imports remain readable.
2. Eight-author metadata exceeded the old 2048 output-token cap. Imports now set a bounded 6144-token per-request budget; ordinary dialogue stays 2048, with the same shared deadline. Malformed metadata protocol can be corrected once within the existing two-attempt budget. No fallback Mock success or partial version is claimed. Safe known-field diagnostics retain failed usage without prompts/raw provider output.
3. PDF wrapping, nonbreaking spaces and glyph spacing broke literal metadata matching. The server maps whitespace-only proposals back to exact raw Unicode quotes/values; changed letters, punctuation, numbers and places remain invalid. It preserves raw spelling, including split words/initials, for human correction. The same whitespace-only source-span alignment fixes an observed real Q&A citation failure; citations still reference already issued sources and store exact raw text and current version/hash.
4. Multiline structured abstracts were reduced to a first paragraph or omitted. Explicit Abstract/摘要 boundaries now preserve the entire bounded span up to Keywords/Introduction; unknown/no reliable closing boundary stays unknown. Summary-only/unstyled cases remain manual rather than relabelled introductory prose.
5. A **Word derivative we made lost author affiliation markers**, and the model misassigned one institution and omitted another. The derivative was repaired from canonical JATS markers; explicit byline numbers are now checked deterministically. A follow-up guard initially mistook `9th Floor` for a label; it now only considers the byline's actual affiliation identifiers. Both defects have regressions. Absence of a numbering link is not a general proof of association; human review remains necessary.
6. A model response omitted eight explicitly available contact emails. Deterministic completion now uses bounded, explicitly named contact blocks, stopping at other author/section blocks including authors omitted by the model. Conflicting contacts stay unknown. Initial-based contributions are left to the responsible editor rather than guessed; they are optional, not required for version confirmation.
7. Grounded Q&A initially repeated older questions because history was presented as consecutive unanswered user messages. History now has completed user/assistant pairs, the last question is authoritative, scientific qualifiers must be preserved, and the last tool round explicitly transitions to respond without extra budget. Incomplete-coverage Chinese absence statements cannot masquerade as evidence and may be corrected once. Review also caught `John` being rewritten as `Johnson` and `smaller studies` as fewer studies; narrow source-based name/comparison guards and correction tests now reject those observed classes. They are not a complete semantic verifier. Historical wrong answers are retained; later correction rounds are separate, and a reviewed dossier does not mean its AI conclusions were human-approved.
8. Independent Word export found 11 missing **displayed bullet prefixes**, with their substantive paragraph text retained. This is disclosed through a specific list-marker warning; automatic list-number/bullet layout is not reconstructed. The audit keeps the 11 raw mismatches and separately shows zero remaining unmatched body paragraphs after the visible bullet prefix is removed. We do not call this 100% layout-faithful extraction.

## Failure history and limits

Failed candidates remain failed/audited; previous successful attempts do not erase the failing runs. Initial cases hit output limits, invalid tool shape, exact evidence failures, contribution overlength and the temporary address-label false positive. After the actual first body answer succeeded, the institution question failed on raw whitespace; it rolled back with no answer before correction. A focused recheck also hit the final-read-round budget and now receives an explicit respond-only transition; no extra round was added. Two legacy tests assumed the feedback was always the last message; they now locate the actual respond-tool message and retain all evidence/budget/no-mutation assertions. A resumed browser driver initially used chunk text with absolute metadata-field offsets; the driver now uses the complete saved field. It reuses committed answers and already confirmed versions without duplicate approvals. These driver errors are distinguished from product failures.

Five full regressions were stopped after further real-paper findings changed source; their interrupted logs are not passes. The delivery run is recorded separately. No tests were removed or database histories deleted.
